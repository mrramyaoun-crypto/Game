# Six physical game screens

All game backdrops and the physical-piece atlas were recovered from the interrupted image-generation session, without regenerating them. Their approved reference sheets are in design/approved/.

Backdrops retain physical framing, grain, ivory paper, ceramics, brass tools and antique ornaments. They contain no game states or score values. Kotlin Android views draw the real puzzle numbers, pieces, counters, selections and button text and receive native touch input.

The atlas provides cream, teal, ochre, terracotta and navy ceramics, raised sandstone, ivory paper, a sepia compass card back, metal mines, brass flags and tool pieces. Relic faces use the existing 16 antique illustrations. Small collection artwork is extracted from approved Mosaic Restoration sample A.

Interactions remain native: fixed six-color Mastermind palette; slot selection; animated relic flips; stroke painting and crosses; Sudoku notes and direct keypad; minefield pan/pinch/flags/chording; sliding 2048 pieces and persisted undo.

The approved home scene and owl launcher artwork are unchanged. The original Enigma stays in its recovered module.
